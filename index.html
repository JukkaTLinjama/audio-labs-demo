Research focus: compare the preferred loudness difference between leaked headrest program content and a shared background scene. The initial headrest-to-neighbour attenuation is set to −15 dB. This prototype studies content-dependent ΔLU while adding a separate simulated cabin-noise layer. Ambient is an environmental condition; Scene is intentionally added background content. Absolute SPL calibration and adaptive control are still intentionally omitted. 
Choose Program A and a Scene bed. Cabin ambient defaults to generated warm brown noise (800 Hz).
